# COEXIST — AI Engine Documentation

## Overview

The COEXIST AI Engine is designed to analyze ecological and infrastructure data to support sustainable urban planning and wildlife coexistence.

The system combines:

* geospatial intelligence
* ecological analysis
* predictive risk scoring
* infrastructure impact simulation
* environmental recommendation systems

to evaluate ecological consequences before infrastructure development begins.

---

# Core Objectives

The AI Engine is designed to:

* identify ecological risk zones
* detect habitat fragmentation
* analyze wildlife corridor disruption
* simulate infrastructure impact
* generate sustainable planning recommendations
* support AI-assisted ecological decision-making

---

# AI Components

## 1. Ecological Risk Scoring Engine

This module evaluates ecological sensitivity using multiple weighted environmental parameters.

### Risk Factors

* forest overlap
* wildlife corridor proximity
* biodiversity hotspot presence
* protected zone intersection
* forest degradation intensity
* infrastructure density
* terrain sensitivity

### Example Logic

| Condition                            | Risk Level |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------- |
| Highway intersects elephant corridor | Critical   |
| Near protected forest                | High       |
| High forest degradation region       | Medium     |
| Low ecological overlap               | Low        |

The system combines these factors into:

* Ecological Sensitivity Score
* Corridor Disruption Score
* Biodiversity Risk Score
* Sustainability Score

---

## 2. Wildlife Corridor Analysis

The AI evaluates:

* wildlife observation clusters
* movement continuity
* corridor bottlenecks
* fragmentation zones

Data Sources:

* biodiversity observations
* GIS layers
* ecological hotspot mapping
* terrain analysis

Outputs:

* probable migration corridors
* corridor disruption regions
* high-risk crossing zones

---

## 3. Infrastructure Impact Simulation

The system simulates ecological impact for:

* highways
* railways
* industrial zones
* smart city projects
* urban expansion

The engine analyzes:

* overlap with ecological regions
* fragmentation probability
* wildlife disruption risk
* environmental stress increase

---

## 4. Recommendation Engine

The AI Recommendation Engine generates:

* alternate route suggestions
* wildlife crossing recommendations
* ecological buffer zones
* sustainable mitigation strategies

Example:
If a proposed highway intersects a wildlife corridor:

* suggest route adjustment
* recommend wildlife underpass
* estimate ecological risk reduction

---

## 5. Forest Monitoring & Encroachment Detection

The system uses:

* satellite imagery
* forest loss data
* disturbance alerts
* land-use change detection

to identify:

* ecological degradation
* probable encroachment
* fragmentation expansion
* vegetation loss
